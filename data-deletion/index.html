1. How to Request Data Deletion

If you have previously submitted personal information through our website—for example, via the contact form or a direct email inquiry—you may request that we delete that information from our records.

To submit a data deletion request, send an email to:

Data Deletion Request

Email: connect@primecoreinfo.com

Subject line: Data Deletion Request

In your email, please include:

  • Your full name as it was provided in your original inquiry.
  • The email address used when contacting us or submitting the form.
  • A brief description of the data you would like deleted (e.g., “Delete my contact form submission from [approximate date]”).

2. Verification of Identity

To protect your privacy, we will verify your identity before processing any deletion request. Verification is performed by confirming that the deletion request originates from the same email address associated with the original submission.

If the request is sent from a different email address, we may ask for additional information to confirm your identity before proceeding.

3. Timeline & Confirmation

We aim to process all verified data deletion requests within 30 days of receiving the request. Once your data has been deleted, we will send a confirmation email to the address from which the request was made.

Please note:

  • Deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed.
  • If your data has already been deleted as part of our standard retention practices, we will inform you accordingly.
  • We do not charge any fee for processing deletion requests.

4. Scope of Deletion

Data deletion covers personal information stored in our internal systems as a result of your website inquiry, including:

  • Contact form submission records (name, email, company, phone, message).
  • Associated internal notification emails generated by the submission.

Deletion does not affect:

  • Publicly available information that was not submitted through our website.
  • Anonymized or aggregated data that cannot be linked back to you.
  • Data we are required to retain for legal or regulatory compliance.

5. Contact & Support

If you have questions about the deletion process, your privacy rights, or our data practices, please contact us:

Primecore Info Systems Pvt. Ltd.

Email: connect@primecoreinfo.com

Website: primecoreinfo.com

For our complete data practices, please review our Privacy Policy and Data Protection Statement.
